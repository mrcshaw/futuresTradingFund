// The script uses an RSI and Bollinger Bands entry, with a session filter that closes all positions outside the chosen New York hours.
// It opens a long when RSI goes above 50 and close goes above the lower Bollinger Band, and it sets the long stop at the lower Bollinger Band.
// It opens a short when RSI goes below 50 and close goes below the upper Bollinger Band, and it sets the short stop at the upper Bollinger Band.
// It also closes all positions if the open P&L reaches the dollar stop from the risk inputs.
